# Product Expiry and Demand Intelligence System

An intelligent inventory management platform designed to help supermarkets, grocery stores, and pharmacies reduce product wastage through expiry risk prediction, demand forecasting, and smart inventory recommendations.

## Overview

Retail businesses often experience financial losses because products expire before being sold, excess inventory accumulates, and customer demand is difficult to predict.

The Product Expiry and Demand Intelligence System aims to address these challenges by analysing product information, expiry dates, current stock levels, and historical sales data.

The system will identify products at risk of remaining unsold before expiry, forecast future demand, and recommend suitable actions such as offering discounts, adjusting purchase quantities, and transferring stock between branches.

## Objectives

- Reduce wastage of expired and unsold products.
- Predict future product demand using historical sales data.
- Identify products with a high risk of expiry.
- Recommend inventory adjustments and stock transfers.
- Improve stock utilisation and reduce financial losses.
- Support data-driven inventory management decisions.

## Key Features

- **Inventory Management:** Manage products, stock quantities, purchase details, and expiry dates.
- **Expiry Risk Prediction:** Identify products that may remain unsold before their expiry dates.
- **Demand Forecasting:** Estimate future product demand using historical sales patterns.
- **Smart Discount Recommendations:** Suggest discounts for products approaching expiry.
- **Stock Transfer Recommendations:** Identify opportunities to transfer excess stock between branches.
- **Inventory Optimisation:** Recommend suitable purchase quantities.
- **Wastage Analysis:** Analyse product wastage and estimate potential financial losses.
- **Analytics Dashboard:** Visualise inventory status, expiry risks, and demand trends.
